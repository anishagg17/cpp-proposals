================================================================
Addendum — 16 Oct 2026: movable_box self-assignment fix
================================================================

The movable_box bug found in the drop_last_while header was also
present in this copy: the assignment operators stored a
non-assignable predicate (a capturing lambda) by destroying it and
reconstructing from the destroyed object, so self-assigning a view
corrupted its predicate (use-after-free under AddressSanitizer).

Changes made on 16 Oct 2026 (both copies of the implementation):
1. impl/views_trim.hpp: both movable_box assignment operators
   (copy and move) now start with the self-assignment guard
   `if (this == std::addressof(other)) return *this;`, matching
   the standard's movable-box. <memory> included for
   std::addressof.
2. impl/test_views_trim.cpp: new regression test — a trim_while
   view whose predicate is a lambda capturing a 64-char
   std::string (heap allocated) is self-copy-assigned and
   self-move-assigned, then iterated and checked.
3. Makefile and build.sh: the Clang binary is no longer assumed
   to be a specific name; both detect clang++ first and fall
   back to clang++-18.

Re-verification after the fix (16 Oct 2026, run in this staged
copy): the full suite passes on g++ 13.3.0 and clang++ 18.1.3 at
-std=c++20 and -std=c++23 (-Wall -Wextra -Werror), and ASan+UBSan
(-fsanitize=address,undefined -fno-sanitize-recover=all) runs are
clean under both compilers at both standards. `make test` and
`./build.sh` both pass end to end.
