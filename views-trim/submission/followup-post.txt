Hi all,

Quick update on views::trim. It now has a number, P4398R0, and I'm
submitting it for the October mailing.

What changed since the first draft:

- The predicate form is now views::trim_while instead of trim_if.
  Thanks to Arthur for the suggestion.
- I fixed a few bugs in the reference implementation: cached iterators
  were being copied along with the view, capturing lambdas didn't work
  as predicates, and composing closures didn't work in C++23.
- The proposed behaviour hasn't changed.

On Hewill's point about use cases: the paper now compares trim with
other languages and libraries, but I haven't done a code-search survey
yet. I plan to add one in R1.
